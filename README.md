## 🔬 Master's Thesis — Population Genomics of *Paradisaea*

**Swedish Museum of Natural History / University of Skövde**

My Master's thesis focused on the **population genomic analysis of the bird-of-paradise genus *Paradisaea*** using genomic data from museum specimens.

### What I worked on

* Analyzed Illumina sequencing data from **85 *Paradisaea* specimens**.
* Performed sequencing quality assessment and reference-based genomic analysis.
* Conducted **mitochondrial and nuclear phylogenetic analyses** to investigate evolutionary relationships.
* Used **PCA and population-structure/admixture analyses** to investigate genetic differentiation and relationships among populations.
* Investigated patterns of **gene flow and population structure** among *Paradisaea* species.
* Interpreted genomic results in the context of species differentiation, evolution and biogeography.

### My contribution

The analysis was based on **published bioinformatics workflows and scripts provided by my supervisor**. I applied and adapted the established workflows to the thesis dataset, performed the downstream analyses, generated and interpreted the results, and investigated the biological questions addressed by the study.

> Original published scripts are not redistributed in this repository. The relevant methods and source publications are cited in the thesis.

📄 **Thesis:** [Population genomic analyses of the bird-of-paradise genus *Paradisaea*](https://his.diva-portal.org/smash/record.jsf?pid=diva2:1832235)
